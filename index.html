"I love card games. I am absolutely terrible at keeping score."

After one too many arguments about whether that last round of Hearts pushed someone over 50, I decided to just build the thing. Not a generic score counter with a plus button — but a proper, game-aware score keeper that knows the rules, the variants, and the house rules your family has been arguing about for decades.

LazyTally started as a personal project for my own game nights. It supports a handful of games right now — the ones I play the most — but the plan is to keep expanding. If your favourite game isn't in there yet, it probably will be.

It's free. It has no accounts. It doesn't need the internet to work. It just quietly keeps score while you enjoy the game.
