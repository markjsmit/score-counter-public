Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

LazyTally ("the app") is a score-keeping app for card and board games. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

Game data (player names, scores, game history) is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

The one exception is the Spectate feature: when you share a live game via QR code, the current game state (player names and scores) is temporarily uploaded to Firebase so a second device can follow along in real time. This data is automatically deleted when the game session ends. It is never used for any other purpose.

Camera Permission

The app requests access to your camera solely to scan QR codes for the Spectate feature. No photos or video are recorded, stored, or transmitted. Camera access is only active while you are actively using the QR scanner inside the app.

Advertising

The app may display banner advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We do not use any third-party analytics or crash reporting services.

Children

LazyTally is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page.
